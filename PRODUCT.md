# Product

## Register

product

## Users

This is a personal health tracker used primarily by Peter on an iPhone. It is opened many times a day, often one-handed, to record food, water, exercise, resistance training, weight, and body composition. Peter has presbyopia, so readable text takes priority over maximum information density.

## Product Purpose

Health Plan turns frequent health logging into a short, dependable mobile workflow. It should make today's remaining calorie budget immediately understandable, keep AI-assisted entry editable, preserve historical records, and make longer-term trends easy to inspect without weakening the speed of daily logging.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, encouraging. The interface should feel like a trustworthy personal instrument rather than a medical portal, fitness competition, or lifestyle campaign.

## Anti-references

- Do not look like a marketing landing page or generic SaaS dashboard.
- Do not use wellness-pastel decoration, glassmorphism, gradients, oversized headings, or ornamental animation.
- Do not create a wall of equally prominent cards or hide frequent actions behind menus.
- Do not let color variety compete with data meaning.
- Do not change storage keys, calculation rules, AI request behavior, or existing logging workflows during visual work.

## Design Principles

1. Show today first: the remaining budget and the next logging action must be obvious at a glance.
2. Keep logging within one clear action: food, water, and exercise remain directly available.
3. Preserve trust in data: values, units, dates, history, and edits must remain consistent across every view.
4. Reveal complexity progressively: advanced analysis, exports, settings, and historical detail remain available without crowding daily use.
5. Prefer familiar mobile controls: repeated workflows should feel predictable and work comfortably with one hand.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Keep compact supporting text at least 14px and frequently used labels or body text at least 16px. Keep touch targets near or above 44 by 44 CSS pixels, allow browser zoom and text scaling, support reduced motion, maintain keyboard and screen-reader operation, and never rely on color alone to communicate state.
